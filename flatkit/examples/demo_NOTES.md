# demo.tpl: the plan (a worked example: learn the craft, never the content)

Topic of the example only: "Why do onions make you cry?" (19 s, 7 shots). Your Short gets its own subject and cast.

| # | time | in | device | planes (far / subject / near) | what moves in the hold |
|---|------|----|--------|-------------------------------|------------------------|
| 1 | 0-2.2 | cut (frame 0) | hook: the claim as banner + title over a close-up face filling the width | gradient + soft white glow + pale bokeh / the face / none | tears fall, blink |
| 2 | 2.2-5.0 | whip up | talking object on a two-colour backdrop disc, casting its whole silhouette on it | gradient + stage beams + far bokeh / the object / dark audience heads, blurred | dance, lids, name banner grows in |
| 3 | 5.0-8.0 | reveal (the onion scene shrinks into the circle above the head) | person, medium shot cropped by the bottom edge, inset circle above the head, fact pills | gradient + pale far discs / the person / none | pills slide in 4 frames apart on their words, blink |
| 4 | 8.0-11.0 | into (the camera dives through that circle) | dark glow scene: the micro world | dark gradient + vignette / glowing molecule with rays / bright bokeh in three planes | molecule pops and turns, banner, caveat plate |
| 5 | 11.0-13.5 | bars | a crowd as a statistic, the share in a second colour | flat yellow ground / the grid / none | rows fill in, the number pops (the one overshoot) |
| 6 | 13.5-16.0 | dissolve | body diagram: profile head cropped by the top, organs packed, the food path ringed in yellow | dark ground + soft bokeh / the body / none | food dots run along the tube and the gut, counter rolls |
| 7 | 16.0-19.0 | cut | a place in planes: far mountains pale and close to the sky, nearer hills darker, sad rain clouds | sky + far hills (blurred) / pond, sun, clouds / none | clouds drift and rain, title pops |

Rules the example follows:
- One idea per shot; every shot starts on a sentence start; idea changes in the measured mix (section 23: about 1 in 4 a cut,
  the rest linked: a reveal pulls back to show the old scene inside the next one, a zoom-through dives INTO the thing the next
  shot shows, bars wipe across).
- Depth in every shot: a far plane, the crisp subject, near-lens shapes where the scene allows; heroes on backdrops cast
  their silhouette (`shadowed`); dark scenes glow.
- Characters: `person`/`head` defaults are the approved construction; change colours, hair style, parting, clothes, mood.
- Organs: lungs, liver, stomach, heart, gut drawn back to front, packed; the tract's yellow ring in two passes
  (`pass: 'edge'` for all tract organs, then `pass: 'fill'`), the tube drawn before the heart so the heart covers it.
- Text pieces land on the words that say them; the caption band (about 0.74-0.83 of the height) stays free.
- Every hold: ONE act on its word (the worry drops on shot 3's entrance, the tag opening then the plate wiping on in shot 5, the
  heart beating in shot 6) over the built-in idle of the people (breathing, head bob, eye darts with `dart: true`, blinks).
