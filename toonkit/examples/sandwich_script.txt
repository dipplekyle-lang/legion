A diner owner puts a ninety-dollar sandwich at the top of his menu and cheers every time nobody orders it. The cook asks what's even in it, and the owner just says, nothing, it's not for eating. He prints it in a big gold box, and customers laugh at it, point at it, then order the eighteen-dollar burger right under it without blinking, and by Friday that burger outsells everything. Then one night a stranger actually orders it, so the owner wraps a grilled cheese in gold foil and serves it with a bow. You see, one crazy price makes everything under it look cheap. So his best salesman is a sandwich nobody buys.
