# Prism  (style key: `3d`)

> True stories rebuilt in 3D.

## What it is

The places, ships and crowds of a true story rebuilt in 3D, directed and checked before it renders.

Real stories retold as a premium 3D documentary: the places, the ships, the crowds and the people of the story rebuilt in real-time 3D faceless white figures in era-correct clothes, real light, fog and weather, drone moves through the scene. Claude directs every shot from the narration, looks at preview stills of each one and fixes what is wrong before the final render on this computer's graphics card. Quiet 2D graphics carry the numbers and dates, maps the routes, real footage the rest.

## Use it when / avoid it when

- **Use it when:** A true story where the place, a ship or a crowd is the point and there is no usable footage.
- **Avoid it when:** Anything that needs real faces or real footage. Check the first render before relying on it.

## What it needs

- A text model: Claude Code (your plan) writes the script and plans the edit. When its limit is reached the engine stops cleanly and says so.
- A voice service. The style's own is algrow (Jon Warm & Grounded Storyteller); if it fails, WaveSpeed is tried next, then ai33, then the computer's own voice (free, robotic).
- The 3D engine (Chromium with WebGL).
- Map scenes use the studio's own map data (no key).
- Chromium (Playwright) and ffmpeg on the machine; both are installed by Legion's setup.

## What it costs

- Style's own estimate: the voice is about $0.05 a minute; Claude's directing runs on your Claude Code plan (a 10-minute video uses about 5 % of a Claude Max 5x week); the 3D renders on this computer, about 15-20 minutes per minute of video.
- Render time is about 12x the length of the video.
- Every paid step is written in the log with its price. With AI images off, nothing is drawn and no image provider is charged.

## How it looks in a Short (rules that apply to every style)

- 1080x1920, 5 to 180 seconds.
- Captions: all lowercase, heavy type, a warm yellow on the word being said, one key word in every other caption emphasised. (`captions.keep_case` / `captions.keep_font` in the style turn these off.)
- No corner labels (name / place / date tags) on the footage.
- A still photo stays on screen at most 3.4 seconds and moves (a slow pan or push).
- When a stretch has no real footage: a bold card over a real photo, or a code-drawn animation placed on the spoken verb.
- Graphics: the documentary graphics, drawn for 9:16.
- It uses the default caption.

## Specific to this style

- Renders in 3D inside the studio's own Chromium pages; it is slow (render time about 12x the video length).

## Status

- NOT tested in the beta work. It has its own 3D pipeline (the threed feature).

## If something goes wrong

See ENGINE.md, section 'Failures and what they mean'. The most common: Algrow paused (voice falls back), no real footage (cards carry the Short), the Claude limit reached (the run stops with a clear message).
