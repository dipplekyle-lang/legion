# Clay parables — measured study (2026-10-09)

Three reference Shorts, 9:16, 720×1280, 24 fps, 67–78 s, mastered at −14 LUFS (all three identical: one pipeline).

## Look (every frame)
- Medium: stop-motion **plasticine clay** miniatures. Visible fingerprints, tool marks and soft lumps on skin and
  clothes; matte clay, never shiny CGI plastic. Props are real-material miniatures (copper, wood, paper, chalk).
- Characters: big rounded heads, **heavy half-closed eyelids** (sleepy, deadpan), white eyeballs with small black
  pupils, large bulbous noses, small mouths, chunky hands. Terracotta / tan skin. Same design language for every
  character; costumes tell who they are (turban, monk robe, tricorn hat, apron).
- Light: warm tungsten key, soft fill, gentle rim; night scenes go deep blue with warm practical lamps.
- Lens: macro / miniature, **shallow depth of field** (backgrounds soft), low camera at character eye level.
- Palette: warm terracotta + one saturated complementary per story (teal floor, orange robes, red coat, blue night).
- Sets: detailed miniature dioramas (palace hall, dirt road, 1700s tavern, ship deck, modern office for the twist).

## Motion (measured)
- Shot length: **0.9 s** (video 1), **2.0 s** (video 2), **1.7 s** (video 3). Hard cuts only, no transitions.
- Inside a shot, characters really move (a foot sinks into mud, a hand stirs syrup, a head turns).
- **Animated on twos**: the frame difference alternates big / ~0 every other frame (25–39% of in-shot frames are
  exact holds) → effectively 12 drawings per second inside a 24 fps file. This is the stop-motion feel.
- Some shots are a slow, smooth camera push or pan instead (continuous diffs) — about 1 in 5.
- Shot types cycle: wide establishing → medium two-shot → **extreme close-up of an object or hand** (chalk, cup,
  foot, finger) → face close-up reacting. Insert close-ups of hands/objects are ~30% of shots.

## Captions
- Bold rounded serif (Cooper Black–like), **golden yellow (#F2C200)**, thin dark shadow, no box.
- Small: ~40 px cap height on 720 wide. Centred, at **~78% of the height** (lower third, above the UI).
- 2–4 words per card, 1–2 lines, changes with the narration, read word-for-word from the voiceover.

## Story structure (all three)
1. **Old parable / historical anecdote** told in present tense ("A king drags a piece of chalk across his great hall").
2. Characters try and fail; the wise one does something simple and surprising.
3. **The twist to modern life** (video 2: "When someone next to you starts to look…" → office, phone, measuring tape).
4. **One-line lesson** ("So stop rubbing at theirs.") on a close-up.
5. End: a finger taps a round profile badge (follow cue), ~1.5 s.

## Voice and sound
- One calm, warm storyteller narrator, present tense, short plain sentences, small pauses.
- Soft background music under the voice; no hard SFX hits. Loud and clean: −14 LUFS.

## Keep / change
- KEEP: clay look, sleepy-eyed character design, on-twos motion, shot lengths, caption treatment, parable → modern
  twist → lesson structure, follow-badge ending.
- CHANGE: the story, characters, setting, the lesson, no copying of their characters, words or channel badge.
