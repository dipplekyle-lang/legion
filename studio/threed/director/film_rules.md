THE FILMMAKER'S RULES FOR THIS FILM (NOCTURNE — 3D night reconstructions cut against real black-and-white archive).
The bar is the hand-made Anthropoid sample the founder called "extremely good": 34 s, 9 3D shots + 8 archival shots, 3D shots
1.7-3.4 s, the void 4.2 s, EVERY shot moving, an FPV one-take drop of three hidden cuts, archival bursts of 0.8-1.3 s
on the named people and events, the dossier ONCE for 3.35 s on the name, the red-slit void ONCE on the menace line, a
typed title — and EIGHT DIFFERENT WORLDS in nine 3D shots (the bomber in the cloud, the hold, the free fall, the
homeland below, the canopies, the desk, the rail tracks in the snow, the void, the title). the founder: "be mega creative in
this style, like this sample, don't limit yourself, make more of these — you are simply an extremely good filmmaker".
His rejected run: maps held 4-5 s, a desk held ~15 s in a row with blank papers, radar scopes as blown-out glow balls,
smoke as stacked spheres, a toy warship, six props on the same black stage. These rules OVERRIDE the generic pacing
further down wherever they differ; a validator measures every one of them and the review must fix every breach.
- R1 PACE: shots 2-3.5 s (the average of every moment 2-3.5 s); nothing over 5 s except ONE judgement hold in the void
  (<= 6 s) per minute. The cold open cuts fastest (2-3 s).
- R2 THE DESK IS A SPICE: archive.desk / the dossier <= 3.5 s a shot and NEVER more than 2 desk shots in any 20 s of the
  film (never two in a row). A desk introduces a real person with their real photo, or shows a document the words
  name — once. A number, a vote, a count, a declaration is NOT a desk: make it physical (THE FILM COOKBOOK D21, D22, D24)
  or show the place where it happened.
- R3 MAPS ARE A GLANCE: a map in 3D (a map on the desk or the wall, a route drawing itself on the land) <= 3 s, then
  dive into the place.
- R4 THE VOID IS FOR MENACE (HISTORIC PHOTOS ARE FLAT (the founder's rule): a B&W / sepia / faded / pre-1990 photo is NEVER an archive.relief or archive.float (no depth pop on old photos) — show it as a flat print (archive.photo / desk / wall / frame) under the slit or the lamp; the red-slit void face is figure.bust (the sample's way).): stage.void, archive.relief and figure.bust only on the lines the TREATMENT marks as
  menace / judgement / symbol (<= 6 s, at most one void per ~15 s, never two in a row — the typed title card D37 excepted). The void is NOT a neutral
  studio for props: a phone, a radio, a radar set, a clock live in their REAL place (a hut, a van, an office, a ship's
  bridge: interior.* or a built room with its window, its lamp, its operator) — or they are a real archival film.
- R5 CHANGE THE WORLD: never more than 2 consecutive shots in the same set (the same desk, the void, the same room, the
  same aircraft or ship world). Plan the film as a chain of worlds (the treatment's set pieces): sky, sea, hill,
  room, street, desk, film, void, sky...
- R6 FLY: at least one FPV / drone move in every ~10 s of 3D (cloud_tunnel, chase, proximity_pass, freefall_follow,
  fpv_line, orbit_reveal, fpv_flythrough, fpv_dive, reveal_rise, crane_down, orbit, follow, or a `keys` line flown with
  "profile": "fpv"). The cold open is an FPV one-take (hidden `occlude` cuts).
- R7 EVERY SHOT ALIVE: >= 2 moving layers in every shot, named in "layers" (the subject + clouds streaming / mist
  drifting / dust in a beam / a light sweeping / a print sliding in / a second craft / sea / smoke). A held shot still
  moves (a 1-3 %/s push or a 10-20 deg arc).
- R8 PAPERS SPEAK: every doc / folder / telegram on a desk or a wall has its own `title`, `stamp` and 2-4 typed `text`
  lines (`|` = new line) in the story's language and institutions, taken from the narration's facts (never blank,
  never the engine's German defaults "Akte / GEHEIM / Abschrift / TELEGRAMM" unless the file really is German).
- R9 PROOF IS REAL: a named real person, crowd or event is real archival film (a footage slot, when this video has
  real film; a FILMS newsreel playing on a period screen, D41) or a real photo (the dossier, the wall, the frame, the
  relief) — never a lit mannequin. When FILMS lists newsreel shots, each plays ONCE, on the line that names its person
  or event: full frame as the burst (D46, with negative flashes — the sample's rhythm) or on a screen in a world (D41). The 3D shot before
  a burst ends on a held composition (or the same motion as the film's first frame: a match cut), the one after opens
  strong.
- R10 ONE WOW PER 20-30 S, planned in the treatment (a whip onto the truth, a crash zoom on the word, an overtaking
  pass, freeze-and-orbit, a light transition, a dolly zoom) — and nothing else flashy in between.
- R11 HEROES THAT RENDER: no emissive prop as the hero (an oscilloscope, a screen, a lamp built from glowing boxes
  renders as a blown-out white ball): a radar / oscilloscope face uses the kit's screen preset when section 14 lists
  one (prop.build preset radar_scope / radar_ppi), else it is read by its glow on the operator, filmed past his shoulder
  with the screen small and off-centre, or it is real film / a real photo (archive.*). Smoke, fire and explosions are fx.*, the fog.volume plume
  when section 16 lists it, or drifting fog.volume boxes (never stacked spheres); a ship.warship is a far silhouette against the dawn or the
  fire (never a 2-s close-up: it reads as a toy). A ship, an aircraft, a tank or a car the story names is the catalog's
  OWN kind when it has one (THE FILM COOKBOOK I: ship.carrier, aircraft.b25 and aircraft.u2 are built for the deck, the cockpit
  close-ups and the shoot-down) — never boxes and cylinders from prop.build.
- R14 LIGHT THAT READS: every frame puts something BRIGHT behind or around its subject — the sun or the moon 4-15 deg up
  AHEAD of the lens (look.sun / look.moon `az` ~ the direction the lens looks, the disc itself 20-30 deg off the axis
  or hidden behind the subject — in frame it blooms: the subject a silhouette against a torn bright sky), moonlit cloud tops or sea or snow under the aircraft, a searchlight beam, a fire glow, a lit
  window, the void's glow. Never a dark subject on a black sky, never flat `overcast` (a grey wash: the Doolittle run 2
  lost its carrier and its bombers in it). A night exterior = the moon ahead of the lens over something that catches
  it; a night interior = one practical light IN the frame (the lamp, the window). The sample's every shot obeys this.
- R12 THE TREATMENT IS THE PLAN: follow its concept, motifs, set pieces, colour script, archival / dossier / void
  windows, the wows and the opening and ending images; give every shot "device": "<the cookbook id it uses>".
- R13 ENGINE TRAPS (seen in this engine's previews, 4 Oct): a fog.volume box bigger than ~10 m, or a dark one, renders as
  a SOLID BLACK BLOCK — a smoke column is the plume (when section 16 lists it) or a pale drifting `layer`; fx.explosion
  grows a mushroom cloud from about a tonne up — a ship's magazine or a bomb hit is yield 200-800 kg, never tonnes or kt
  on a 1940s target; detail.* kinds are tabletop INSERTS that bring their own plank surface / wall (a lifebuoy "on the
  water" rendered on floorboards) — never on the sea or open ground; interior.small_room is a bathroom / closet /
  hallway — a hut, a van or a radio room is built (stage.void floor matte + a prop.build wall with a window light) or
  interior.office / cell / bunker, keys >= 0.6 m from every wall (closer = the lens in the wall, a blank frame).
