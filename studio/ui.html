    Make it yours — a two-minute tutorial
    

      
Nothing here is locked. Every channel is one file in styles/; every graphic, caption style,
        footage source and voice is a setting or a small script you own. Open this folder in Claude Code and say what you want.

      
    
        
  • Your own script. Paste it into Options → Your own script. The video is as long as your script; the
          length field is ignored.
    • 
        
  • Learn from the channels you admire. Paste two or three of their scripts or transcripts into Claude Code and say
          “write our script prompt in this voice”. Claude turns them into prompts.outline and
          prompts.script of your style, and every video after that reads like them.
    • 
        
  • A new channel in a sentence. “Make a true-crime style from DOSSIER, darker, red accent, captions bigger.”
          Claude copies the style file, changes what you named, renders a preview and looks at it before it says done.
    • 
        
  • Anything else. Fonts, colours, where the captions sit, which graphics the director may use, how much
          stock versus YouTube footage, the music, the narrator — all in the style file or in Options.
    • 
        
  • See it first. samples/ holds one finished video per channel; python kits.py preview <kit>
          renders every graphic of a look without spending anything.
    • 
      

      
README → Make it yours has the commands; README-STYLES.md explains every channel.

    

  


  
  

    Sell your own book from your channel
    

      
Your presenter already teaches something — sell the finished version of it. Open this folder in Claude Code
        and say “build a product for my channel”. It makes, in about an hour:

      
    
        
  • The book — 60 numbered, illustrated lessons in a PDF (what you need, the steps with real numbers, why it
          works, how people ruin it, where it doesn't hold), a problem index and a one-page cheat sheet. Fact-checked.
    • 
        
  • The page that sells it — a long landing page, terms and refund pages, a Stripe checkout and a download page,
          ready to publish on Vercel. A launch guide PDF walks you through Stripe, Vercel and your domain.
    • 
        
  • The videos sell it for you — the PITMASTER style (or any style you attach the book to) mentions a real
          lesson halfway through, shows the book and a card with a QR code, and puts the video's own short link first in the
          description, so Stripe shows which video sold each copy.
    • 
      

      
README-OFFER.md has the commands; offer/PLAYBOOK.md is how it should look.
