# MENTOR — he teaches it, the screen builds around him

A photoreal presenter teaches from a dim modern boardroom, and the graphics live **inside his shot**: glossy glass cards
slide past him at table height, a neon countdown hangs in front of him, a glass badge beside his head drains to a
percentage, his example prompt types itself into a chat bar. The edit never stands still — wide, medium, close, slow
pushes in and out cut on sentence starts — and between his shots a 2D cartoon of him acts the story out with a moving
camera and motion blur, next to big glass objects and real things turning in the light.

## Use it for
Teaching and "how to" channels: AI tools, productivity, business skills, money — anything a mentor explains to camera.

## What it needs
- The AI presenter (Kling lip-sync through WaveSpeed or kie): a presenter from the library (the sample uses **theo**,
  a fictional host) or your own photo. Upscaling before close crops makes the medium and close shots sharp.
- Claude draws the cartoon shots (about 2 minutes of Claude per shot).
- Cost: about **$2.50 a minute** (presenter + upscaling), plus Claude on your plan.

## The look
- Overlays (cards, countdown, meter, prompt, path) are rendered with transparency and composited over the presenter
  after the splice, each with its own sound.
- Full-frame moments: glass objects, tier cards, real objects, and the cartoon of the presenter.
- Captions are off (the graphics carry the words); music, a room tone on the voice, and a sound for every graphic.
