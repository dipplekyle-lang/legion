Legion Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

The short version. Legion is software that runs on your own computer. We, the makers of Legion, do not run servers for it, do not have accounts, and do not receive your data. Your YouTube sign-in, your channel data and everything you make in Legion stay on your computer. If you choose to connect outside services (for example an image provider), Legion sends them only what is needed to do the job you asked for.

1. Who we are

Legion is a YouTube packaging tool (thumbnails, titles and Shorts) that you install and run on your own computer. "We" and "us" mean the makers of Legion. You can reach us at [your contact email].

2. What Legion stores, and where

Everything Legion stores is kept in the Legion folder on your computer. None of it is sent to us. This includes:

  • your channel details and the settings you choose;
  • the thumbnails, titles, Shorts and projects you make;
  • what Legion learns about which videos perform well;
  • your API keys for the services you connect, and your Google sign-in token.

You can delete any of it at any time by deleting the Legion folder, or the output folder and .env file inside it.

3. Signing in with Google (YouTube)

If you choose Sign in with Google, Legion asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube channel:

  • youtube.readonly, to see your channel and your videos;
  • yt-analytics.readonly, to see your videos' statistics, such as views, impressions and click-through rate.

Legion uses this only to show you how your own videos perform and to learn what works for your channel. It never uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything, and it cannot. The sign-in token is stored only on your computer and is only ever sent to Google. We never see it.

Legion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Legion uses YouTube API Services; by using that feature you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

To disconnect: press Disconnect in Legion (Settings → Your channel), or remove Legion's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the token from your computer.

4. Public YouTube information

To find what is working in your niche, Legion reads publicly available YouTube pages (such as public channel pages, public videos, their titles, thumbnails and view counts) directly from your computer. It does not read anyone's private data.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics, tracking or usage data from Legion.
  • We do not sell, share or rent your data. We do not have it.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not show ads.

7. The Legion website and waitlist

This policy covers the Legion software. If you join our waitlist or buy Legion on our website, we receive the details you give us there (such as your email address) only to contact you about Legion and to handle your purchase. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

8. Children

Legion is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security

Because your data stays on your computer, keeping it safe depends mostly on your computer's own security. Legion only talks to the services described above, over encrypted connections.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and describe important changes on this page.
